# AORA UX and Product Review

AORA is an operations product; frequent actions should be obvious and low-friction.

Evaluate:
- primary job-to-be-done per role
- information hierarchy
- number of decisions and interactions
- sensible defaults/pre-fill
- destructive-action clarity and undo/recovery
- loading, empty, validation, error, success and stale states
- mobile viewport and touch targets
- keyboard/focus/semantic accessibility
- whether permissions are understandable without leaking unauthorized data
- whether realtime changes appear without manual refresh when the workflow expects it

For high-frequency workflows, aim for <=3 primary interactions when domain rules allow it, but never hide a required safety/review step merely to hit a click target.

Use competitor evidence as input, not as the design spec.
